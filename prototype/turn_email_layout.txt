Subject: [ATFL 3f2a9c11] Above the Fog Line

Day 1 · 07:00 · morning

Morning. 7:00.

You come over the ridge and the world drops away: below you, an ocean
of fog, still as held breath, reaching every horizon. The trail is
empty. It has been empty all morning.

Your cheek is wet. One red drop, like rain — except it isn't raining.
The sky above you is clear.

At your feet: a water bottle, half full, cap off. Nobody in sight.

The trail goes two ways — switchbacks climbing the ridge behind you,
and one steep descent that drops straight toward the fog.

--- Known places ---
trailhead (where you are)
trail-up — not yet explored
trail-down — not yet explored
fog-below — visible below, not yet visited

Carrying: hands empty · backpack empty

What do you do?
